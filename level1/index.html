Study Plan — Level 1

  • Month 1–2: Study Quant Methods + Economics — get the maths foundation right first. Complete all EOC questions.
  • Month 2–3: Cover Financial Statement Analysis — the heaviest L1 topic. This is where most candidates lose marks.
  • Month 3–4: Equity + Fixed Income — memorise formulas, practice valuation questions every day.
  • Month 4–5: Derivatives + Alternatives + Portfolio — lighter topics. Don't over-invest time here.
  • Month 5: Ethics — dedicate 2 full weeks to Ethics ONLY. 15–20% weight and very learnable.
  • Final 3–4 weeks: 3+ full mock exams. Review weak areas only. No new topics after this point.

Top tips for Level 1

  • ✓ Ethics is 15–20% of L1 — study it last but intensely. Many candidates skip Ethics and regret it.
  • ✓ FSA + Ethics combined = ~35% of your exam. Master these two before anything else.
  • ✓ Do EOC (end-of-chapter) questions after EVERY reading — not just before the exam.
  • ✓ Don't try to memorise everything. Understand the 'why' — L1 tests comprehension, not recall.
  • ✓ Use the CFA Institute question bank — 3,000+ questions in the Learning Ecosystem, included in your fee.
  • ✓ Mock exam timing: 180 questions in 270 minutes = 1.5 minutes per question. Practice timing from Day 1.

Frequently Asked — Level 1

How difficult is CFA Level I for Indian students?

CFA Level I difficulty depends on your background. Commerce/CA students find FSA and Ethics easier. Engineers find Quant and Fixed Income easier. The difficulty is manageable with 300–320 hours of structured study. The global pass rate is 39–44%, but Indian candidates with finance backgrounds often clear it in the 45–55% range. The biggest challenge is time management — 180 questions in 270 minutes at exam pressure.

How many hours should I study for CFA Level I?

CFA Institute recommends 300 hours. Indian candidates who clear L1 first attempt typically report 280–350 hours depending on background. Rule of thumb: 2 hours/day for 5 months = ~300 hours. Add 30–40% buffer if you're new to finance topics.

Can I clear CFA Level I while working full time?

Yes — most Indian CFA candidates work full time while preparing. Typical routine: 1.5–2 hours before or after work on weekdays (8–10 hours/week) + 6–8 hours on Sundays = ~14–18 hours/week = ~280–360 hours over 4–6 months. This is the most common and successful approach.

Which topics should I prioritise in CFA Level I?

Priority order: 1) Financial Statement Analysis (FSA) — 13–17%, hardest topic. 2) Ethics — 15–20%, most learnable. 3) Equity Investments — 10–12%, needs valuation practice. 4) Fixed Income — 10–12%, formula-heavy. These 4 topics cover ~55–65% of the exam. If you master these, you pass.
