Study Plan — Level 3

  • Month 1: Portfolio Management — Individual and Institutional IPS writing. This is unique to L3 and requires specific practice.
  • Month 2: Asset Allocation — strategic, tactical, liability-driven. The heaviest L3 topic cluster.
  • Month 3: Fixed Income portfolio management + Equity strategies. Applied portfolio-level, not security-level.
  • Month 4: Derivatives + Alternatives + Risk Management.
  • Month 5: Ethics (essay questions possible) + full portfolio construction practice.
  • Final 4–5 weeks: 4+ full mock exams with ESSAY GRADING. Grade your own essays against CFA Institute model answers. This is non-negotiable for L3.

Top tips for Level 3

  • ✓ WRITE mock essay answers — do not just read model answers. Typing/writing practice is essential for L3 essay section.
  • ✓ IPS (Investment Policy Statement) questions appear almost every year — master the format cold. Every IPS has the same 7 components.
  • ✓ L3 essay grading is strict about keyword usage — say 'risk tolerance is above average' not just 'they can take more risk'.
  • ✓ The afternoon item set section is similar to L2 — treat it the same way. Morning essay is the real challenge.
  • ✓ Behavioural finance biases are commonly tested in essays — memorise bias names and how to 'moderate' each one.
  • ✓ L3 is the most career-relevant level — the portfolio management concepts you study here are used daily by real portfolio managers.

Frequently Asked — Level 3

How is CFA Level III different from Level I and II?

Level III has two major differences: 1) The morning essay section (constructed response) — you type actual answers rather than choosing from multiple choice. This requires a completely different preparation approach — you need to practice writing CFA-style answers. 2) The entire focus shifts to portfolio management — away from individual security analysis (L1, L2) to portfolio construction, asset allocation, and IPS writing. Most candidates find L3 the most intellectually satisfying level and the most directly applicable to real investment management roles.

What is an IPS in CFA Level III?

IPS = Investment Policy Statement. It's a formal document that defines an investor's return objective, risk tolerance, time horizon, liquidity needs, tax considerations, legal/regulatory constraints, and unique circumstances. IPS questions appear in almost every Level III exam. The key skill is reading a client scenario and translating it into IPS format with specific, justifiable statements. IPS mastery is one of the highest-ROI study areas for Level III.

Pass rate for CFA Level III?

CFA Level III pass rate is approximately 52–56% — the highest of all three levels. This seems counterintuitive (it's the hardest material) but reflects the highly committed, experienced candidate pool who have already cleared L1 and L2. Most L3 failures are due to poor essay technique rather than knowledge gaps — practicing mock essay answers is the most effective preparation strategy.
