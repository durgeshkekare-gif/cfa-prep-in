CFA Study Material India 2026 — What Actually Works

Schweser, Bloomberg, official curriculum, India coaching — honest comparison of what to use, what to skip and how to save money without hurting pass rate.

Bottom line first

The CFA Institute Learning Ecosystem (included in exam fee) is sufficient to pass. Most candidates supplement with Kaplan Schweser Notes for efficiency. Bloomberg has the best question bank. India coaching adds accountability but not knowledge you cannot get cheaper. Schweser + official content = optimal.

Recommended stack — used by most Indian CFA passers

  1. CFA Institute Learning Ecosystem — always primary; included free in exam fee
  2. Kaplan Schweser Notes — read instead of or alongside official curriculum; much faster (~₹25–35K)
  3. 3+ full mock exams (timed) — CFA Institute (2 included) + Schweser practice tests; non-negotiable
Total cost estimate: Exam fee (early) ~₹78,500 + Schweser ~₹30,000 = ~₹1,08,500/level. All 3 levels = ~₹3.25L over 4 years.
