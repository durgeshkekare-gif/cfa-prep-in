Which credential for which career?

Portfolio management, equity research, fund management
Best: CFA
CFA is the global gold standard for investment roles. Most AMCs, hedge funds and equity research firms require or prefer CFA.
Auditing, taxation, Big 4, CA firm, CFO track
Best: CA (ICAI)
CA is India's dominant credential for audit and taxation. ICAI membership is legally required for signing audit reports.
Risk management (market, credit, operational)
Best: CFA + FRM
FRM for risk-specific roles; CFA gives broader investment context. Combination is strongest for senior risk roles at banks.
Investment banking, consulting, general management
Best: MBA (IIM/ISB)
Top MBA for IB/consulting access. Adding CFA L1 strengthens IB deal team applications significantly.
FinTech, quant finance, analytics
Best: CFA + IIM Analytics
CFA gives financial markets foundation; IIM Kozhikode Analytics adds data credential. Demanded by FinTech firms.
BFSI sector broadly in India
Best: CFA L1 + Online MBA Finance
L1 gives CFA Candidate status. Online MBA Finance from Amrita/Manipal adds degree. Most cost-efficient BFSI path.
