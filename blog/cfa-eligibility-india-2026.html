CFA Eligibility India 2026 — Who Can Register and What You Need

CFA eligibility — the short version

CFA is one of the most accessible global finance credentials in terms of entry requirements. There is no entrance exam, no age limit, and no stream restriction.

You are eligible for CFA Level I if you have any ONE of these:

  • A Bachelor's degree in any discipline (engineering, commerce, arts, science, law — any field qualifies)
  • Currently in the final year of a bachelor's degree programme
  • 4,000 hours of professional work experience (accumulated over at least 3 years) — no degree required if this threshold is met

Can engineering graduates do CFA in India?

Yes — absolutely. In fact, engineers are among the most successful CFA candidates in India. Engineering graduates often have stronger quantitative foundations (Quant Methods, Fixed Income, Derivatives) than commerce graduates. The only area where engineers need extra effort is Financial Statement Analysis (FSA) — accounting-heavy topics that are not part of engineering curricula.

Many of India's top portfolio managers and quantitative analysts at AMCs and FinTech firms have engineering backgrounds combined with CFA credentials.

Work experience requirement — for the Charter (not for exams)

This is a common source of confusion: you do NOT need work experience to appear for any CFA exam level. Work experience is only required to receive the actual CFA Charter after passing all 3 levels.

To receive the Charter, you need: 4,000 hours of relevant professional experience in roles directly related to investment decision-making or producing work that informs investment decisions. This can be accumulated before, during, or after your CFA exams — so students who start in final year can accumulate experience concurrently while clearing the 3 levels.

What counts as "relevant experience" for the Charter?

CFA Institute accepts a broad range of roles. Eligible experience includes:

  • Equity research / security analysis
  • Portfolio management (direct or assistant)
  • Investment banking (M&A, ECM, DCM teams)
  • Risk management at financial firms
  • Financial planning and advisory
  • Treasury and corporate finance (if investment-decision related)
  • Quantitative analysis in financial contexts
  • Financial consulting (strategy, valuation work)

Roles that typically do not count: pure accounting, audit, tax compliance, back-office operations, data entry, or HR/admin at financial firms.

Registration process — step by step

  1. Go to cfainstitute.org
  2. Create an account → Fill professional profile
  3. Choose Level I exam window (Feb / May / Aug / Nov)
  4. Pay one-time enrollment fee: ~₹29,200 ($350) (~₹29,200)
  5. Pay exam registration fee: ~₹78,400 ($940) early / ~₹1,04,200 ($1,250) standard
  6. Select test centre (Mumbai, Delhi, Bangalore, Chennai, Hyderabad, Pune, Kolkata, Ahmedabad)
  7. Receive confirmation → access Learning Ecosystem to begin studying
