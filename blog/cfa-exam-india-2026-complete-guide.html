CFA Exam India 2026 — The Complete Guide

What is the CFA?

CFA stands for Chartered Financial Analyst. It is a globally recognised professional credential awarded by the CFA Institute (headquartered in Charlottesville, Virginia, USA). The CFA programme covers investment management, financial analysis, ethics and portfolio management across three progressive exam levels.

Since 1963, over 200,000 professionals across 170+ countries have earned the CFA Charter. India has approximately 12,000+ active charterholders — the 3rd largest country globally after USA and UK.

Eligibility for CFA in India

CFA is among the most accessible global finance credentials — no entrance exam, no specific stream requirement:

  • Education: Bachelor's degree (any discipline) OR final year of bachelor's
  • Alternative: 4,000 hours professional work experience (if no degree)
  • Age: No age limit
  • Stream: Engineering, Commerce, Arts, Science, Law — all qualify
  • Language: English only

India Test Centres 2026

CFA exams in India are conducted at Prometric test centres in: Mumbai, Delhi, Bangalore, Chennai, Hyderabad, Pune, Kolkata, Ahmedabad.

Study Plan Overview

  • Level I: 300–320 hours over 4–6 months. Focus on FSA + Ethics first.
  • Level II: 330–350 hours over 5–7 months. Master vignette reading technique.
  • Level III: 360+ hours over 6–8 months. Essay writing practice is critical.

CFA Career Paths in India

CFA opens doors to: Portfolio Management (₹18–40L), Equity Research (₹8–25L), Investment Banking (₹15–35L), Wealth Management (₹10–28L), Risk Management (₹12–30L), FinTech Analytics (₹15–45L). Charterholders earn 25–45% more than non-CFA peers at equivalent experience.
