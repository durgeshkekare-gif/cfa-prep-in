CFA Salary India 2026 — Complete Role-by-Role Guide

CFA salary India 2026 — by experience level

CFA charterholder salary in India depends heavily on role, experience, employer type (sell-side vs buy-side vs consulting) and city. The numbers below reflect total compensation (base + variable) at market-rate employers in metro cities.

CFA salary premium — what the data shows

According to CFA Institute's annual compensation survey, CFA charterholders earn 25–45% more than non-CFA peers at equivalent experience level and role. For India specifically:

  • At junior analyst level (0–3 yrs): CFA candidate status adds ₹1–2L above non-CFA in the same role
  • At mid-level (3–8 yrs): Charterholder status adds ₹4–8L above non-CFA peers
  • At senior level (8+ yrs): CFA is often a gate-keeper for portfolio management roles — non-CFA candidates may be excluded entirely

Top paying CFA employers India 2026

The highest-paying employers for CFA holders in India are: Goldman Sachs India, Morgan Stanley India, JP Morgan, BlackRock India, Nomura India (global firms — ₹25–60L+ at mid-level), followed by domestic buy-side leaders like HDFC AMC, Mirae Asset, Axis AMC, Kotak Securities, Motilal Oswal (₹15–40L at mid-level).

Does CFA Level I pass increase salary?

Yes — even a Level I pass adds CFA Candidate status to your LinkedIn profile and resume. For entry-level finance hiring in India, this is increasingly valued. Most large equity research houses and AMCs have started filtering candidates by CFA Candidate or Charterholder status for research roles. The full compensation premium comes at Level II pass or Charterholder status, but Level I alone is not valueless.
