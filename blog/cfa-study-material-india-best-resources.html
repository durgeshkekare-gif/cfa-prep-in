Best CFA Study Material India 2026 — What Indian Candidates Actually Use

What study material do Indian CFA candidates actually use?

Based on coaching provider surveys and CFA Institute research, the study material stack used by the majority of Indian CFA passers is: CFA Institute Learning Ecosystem (free) + Kaplan Schweser Notes (~₹25–35K/level). This is not a coincidence — it balances official accuracy with reading efficiency.

Schweser vs CFA Institute Official — what's the difference?

  • CFA Official Learning Ecosystem: The authoritative source. All exam questions are written from official curriculum. ~4,500 pages per level. Comprehensive but long. Includes the digital Q bank (3,000+ questions) and 2 official mock exams — these are included free in your exam fee.
  • Kaplan Schweser Notes: Condensed, efficient version of the official curriculum. ~1,500 pages per level vs 4,500. Same content, shorter. Many candidates read Schweser first, then use Official for unclear topics. Also includes QBank and practice exams (~₹25–35K extra).
  • Verdict: Read Schweser for speed; use Official for depth on unclear topics; always do Official Q bank and mock exams regardless of what notes you use.

India coaching providers — do you need one?

You do not need coaching to pass CFA. The majority of Indian charterholders self-studied. Coaching is valuable for: structured accountability (biggest benefit), doubt resolution, peer study groups, and India-specific exam tips.

Consider coaching if you: previously failed and need a reset; struggle with self-discipline; or specifically want the peer networking from a structured cohort.

Best India coaching providers 2026:

  • IMS Proschool — most established in India; CFA Institute recognised; centres in Mumbai, Delhi, Bangalore, Pune, Hyderabad; ~₹35–55K/level
  • Fintree Finance — strong L2/L3 coaching; good online delivery for working professionals; ~₹30–50K/level
  • Miles Education — CFA Institute Approved Prep Provider; strong in Hyderabad and online; ~₹35–50K/level
  • EduPristine / Finvision — classroom delivery; Mumbai/Delhi focused; ~₹40–60K/level

Recommended study stack — most efficient

  1. CFA Institute Learning Ecosystem — Q bank + 2 official mock exams (FREE)
  2. Kaplan Schweser Notes — primary reading material (~₹25–35K)
  3. Schweser QBank — 4,000+ additional practice questions
  4. 3+ full mock exams under timed conditions (2 official + 1 Schweser)

Total cost: ~₹78,500 (exam, early reg) + ₹30,000 (Schweser) = ~₹1,08,500 per level.
