CFA Level 1 Pass Rate — Global and India Context

CFA Institute publishes global pass rates per exam window. India-specific pass rates are not published separately, but Indian candidates broadly track the global average. The pass rate has ranged from 36% to 44% across 2023-2025 windows — meaning 56-64% of test-takers fail Level 1 on each attempt.

Why November Pass Rates Are Lower

The November window attracts the largest candidate pool globally — many first-time takers schedule November as their primary attempt. A larger and more diverse candidate pool (including underprepared candidates) mathematically lowers the average pass rate. The exam difficulty is standardised across windows via Item Response Theory — the lower November pass rate reflects cohort composition, not harder questions.

How Indian Candidates Can Beat the Average

  • Start 6 months before the window — not 3 months
  • Do NOT skip FRA (Financial Reporting & Analysis) — 13-17% weight, heavy calculation
  • Use the CFA Institute's official question bank (EOC questions) alongside Schweser
  • Complete 3+ full-length mocks (180 questions each, 4.5 hours) in test conditions
  • Score 70%+ on mocks before sitting the exam — candidates who pass typically score 75%+ on mocks
  • Revise Ethics in the final 2 weeks — it's the most-flagged topic for borderline candidates

First Attempt vs Multiple Attempts

CFA statistics show that candidates who pass do so across 1-3 attempts. First-attempt pass probability is approximately 40%. Second-attempt (after one fail): slightly higher as candidates know the format. Candidates who fail twice and persist still have a significant pass rate on the third attempt. The key variable is study hours and quality of preparation — not inherent aptitude.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the CFA Level 1 pass rate in 2025-26?

CFA Level 1 pass rates: Feb 2025: 44%, May 2025: 41%, Aug 2025: 39%, Nov 2025: 36%. The average across 2025 windows is approximately 40%. India-specific pass rates are not published separately but broadly track the global average.

How many people pass CFA Level 1 on the first attempt?

Approximately 40% of first-time CFA Level 1 candidates pass on their first attempt (based on historical global rates). This means roughly 60% fail on the first attempt. However, candidates who study 300+ hours and score 70%+ on full mocks have significantly higher pass rates than the average.

What score do you need to pass CFA Level 1?

CFA Institute does not publish a fixed passing score (MPS — Minimum Passing Score). The MPS is determined after each exam using a standard-setting process. Historically, candidates scoring above 70% on official practice exams have strong pass rates. Scoring below 50% in FRA or Ethics significantly increases fail risk regardless of overall performance.
