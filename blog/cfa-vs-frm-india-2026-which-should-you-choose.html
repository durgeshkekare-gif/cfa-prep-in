CFA vs FRM India 2026 — Which Should You Choose for Your Finance Career?

August 24, 2026 · 7 min read · cfa-prep.in

CFA vs FRM — Two Very Different Finance Credentials

CFA (Chartered Financial Analyst) and FRM (Financial Risk Manager) are the two most globally recognised finance credentials in India, but they serve fundamentally different career tracks. Choosing the wrong one wastes 2-5 years of study time. This guide gives you the honest framework to decide.

Choose CFA if You Are Targeting:

  • Equity research analyst at brokerage or AMC
  • Portfolio manager at mutual fund or PMS
  • Investment banking analyst or associate
  • Wealth management advisor at private bank
  • CFA charterholder is a near-mandatory filter at top AMCs in India

Choose FRM if You Are Targeting:

  • Credit risk or market risk roles at banks (SBI, HDFC, ICICI, Axis, Kotak)
  • Risk management at NBFCs, insurance companies
  • Basel/RBI compliance and risk reporting roles
  • Risk consulting at Big 4 (Deloitte Risk, EY FAAS, KPMG Risk)
  • Quantitative risk analyst at hedge funds or trading desks

Can You Do Both CFA and FRM?

Yes — and the combination is increasingly powerful in senior roles at banks that have both investment and risk functions. A common sequencing: FRM first (18 months, lower cost, opens risk roles immediately) → CFA later (adds investment analysis depth for senior roles). Alternatively: CFA first → FRM for specialisation if moving into risk advisory.

The India Job Market Reality 2026

In India's BFSI sector, CFA charter is most valued at AMCs (Axis, Nippon, Mirae, DSP, PPFAS etc.), broking houses and investment banks. FRM is most valued at risk functions of large banks (SBI, ICICI, HDFC, Axis) and at NBFCs with active risk management frameworks. For government bank and PSU finance roles: neither credential is required but both are strong differentiators.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is CFA or FRM better for banking in India?

FRM is specifically designed for banking and financial risk roles — it directly covers the frameworks used in bank risk departments (Basel, ICAAP, stress testing). CFA is better for investment-side banking (equity research, M&A advisory, portfolio management). For commercial banking and risk roles: FRM. For investment banking and asset management: CFA.

How long does FRM take compared to CFA?

FRM can be completed in 6-18 months (2 parts, both can be taken in the same calendar year). CFA takes 3-5 years minimum (3 levels, each offered limited times per year). FRM is significantly faster to complete, making it attractive for professionals who need the credential in a shorter timeframe.

What is the FRM salary in India 2026?

FRM-certified professionals in India 2026: Risk Analyst (2-4 yrs) ₹8-14L; Credit Risk Manager (5-8 yrs) ₹14-25L; Chief Risk Officer (12+ yrs) ₹40-80L. FRM credential commands 15-25% premium over non-certified peers in risk management roles at large banks and NBFCs.
