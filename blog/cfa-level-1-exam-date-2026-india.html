CFA Level 1 Exam Windows 2026 — India

CFA Level 1 is offered four times per year globally, including in India. The 2026 exam windows are February, May, August, and November. Each window has a registration deadline approximately 3-4 months before the exam date. Prometric test centres in Bangalore, Mumbai, Delhi, Hyderabad, Chennai, Kolkata, and Ahmedabad are active for all windows.

Pass Rate Reality Check

CFA Level 1 global pass rate: approximately 36-44% across 2023-25 windows. India pass rates broadly mirror the global average. The exam is genuinely difficult — 6 months of structured preparation at 12-15 hours/week is the minimum for a realistic pass probability. Most first-attempt failures are due to underestimating FRA and Ethics.

Frequently Asked Questions

When is CFA Level 1 exam in India in 2026?

CFA Level 1 2026 exam windows in India: February (11-17 Feb), May (13-19 May), August (18-24 Aug), and November (18-24 Nov). Exact dates within each window are assigned by Prometric at time of scheduling. India has test centres in Bangalore, Mumbai, Delhi NCR, Hyderabad, Chennai, Kolkata, and Ahmedabad.

How much does CFA Level 1 cost in India in 2026?

One-time enrollment (first-time candidates): $350 (~₹29,000). Early registration: $940 (~₹78,000). Standard registration: $1,250 (~₹1,04,000). Late registration: $1,550 (~₹1,29,000). Total first-time cost at early registration: ~$1,290 (~₹1,07,000). Fees are paid in USD on cfainstitute.org.

How many months to prepare for CFA Level 1?

CFA Institute recommends 300+ study hours for Level 1. At 15 hours/week, this is approximately 5 months. At 10 hours/week, 7 months. Most successful Indian candidates who pass on first attempt study 12-15 hours/week for 5-6 months. Starting 6 months before your target window is the standard recommendation.
