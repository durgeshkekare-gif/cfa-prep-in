CFA After CA India 2026 — Should You Do Both & What Career It Opens

September 03, 2026 · 6 min read · cfa-prep.in

CA + CFA — India's Most Powerful Finance Credential Combination

The combination of CA (ICAI) and CFA (CFA Institute) is increasingly recognised as India's strongest finance credential pairing. CA gives unmatched technical accounting and audit depth. CFA adds investment analysis, portfolio management, and global financial market expertise. Together they create a profile that commands premium roles in BFSI, investment management, corporate finance, and the intersection of all three.

Timeline — Doing CFA After CA

Most CA professionals attempt CFA Level 1 while working in their first 2-3 years post-qualification. The CA articleship builds the accounting and financial reporting foundation that directly helps in CFA FRA (Financial Reporting & Analysis) — the toughest Level 1 topic for non-CA candidates. CA professionals typically score higher in FRA and Ethics than purely technical candidates.

  • Level 1: 4-5 months prep at 12-15 hrs/week — CA background makes FRA significantly easier
  • Level 2: 5-6 months — focus shifts to equity valuation, fixed income — less CA overlap
  • Level 3: 4-5 months — portfolio management and essay format — new territory for most CAs
  • Total: 3-5 years to complete all three levels while working full time

When CA + CFA Is Not the Right Move

  • You want to stay in audit, tax, or statutory compliance — CFA adds little value here
  • You have less than 2 years post-CA experience — build domain first
  • You are already targeting CFO track via IIM route — IIM + CA is more direct for management roles
  • The 3-5 year CFA commitment is difficult alongside a demanding CA job

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I do CFA after CA in India?

Yes — many CAs pursue CFA after qualifying. The CA+CFA combination is increasingly recognised as India's strongest finance credential pairing, particularly for investment management, corporate finance leadership, and BFSI roles. CA's FRA foundation gives a head start in CFA Level 1.

Which is better CA or CFA in India?

They serve different tracks. CA (ICAI) is essential for audit, tax, compliance, and the CFO track in Indian corporate finance. CFA is essential for investment analysis, portfolio management, and equity research. The combination is most powerful — but individually, CA has broader applicability in India's job market.

What salary can CA+CFA expect in India 2026?

CA + CFA professionals earn 40-55% more than CA-only peers in investment and corporate finance roles. At 5-8 years: ₹20-38L. At 10+ years: ₹40-80L. Senior portfolio managers and CFOs with CA+CFA can earn ₹70L-₹2Cr+.
