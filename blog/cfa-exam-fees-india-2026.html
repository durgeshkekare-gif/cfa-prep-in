CFA Exam Fees India 2026 — Complete Guide to All Costs and How to Save

CFA exam fees 2026 — complete breakdown

Understanding the full cost structure of the CFA exam before you register is important — the fees are in USD and INR conversions fluctuate. Here is the complete 2026 fee structure.

India test centres 2026

CFA exams in India are conducted at Prometric authorised testing centres in 8 cities: Mumbai, Delhi/NCR, Bangalore, Chennai, Hyderabad, Pune, Kolkata, Ahmedabad.

Important: Popular centres (Mumbai, Bangalore, Delhi) fill up quickly. Book your test centre when you register — do not wait until your exam window approaches.

How to save on CFA fees — 3 strategies

  • 1. Always use early registration: Save $310 (~₹25,700) per level vs standard registration. Over 3 levels, early registration saves ~₹77,000. This is the single easiest saving.
  • 2. Apply for the Access Scholarship: The CFA Institute Access Scholarship reduces your exam fee to just $350 (~₹29,200) per level — a saving of ₹49,300–₹1,00,100 per exam. Apply at cfainstitute.org under the Scholarships section, before the exam registration window opens. Applications are competitive and fill quickly for India.
  • 3. Use the free Learning Ecosystem fully: The official CFA curriculum, digital Q bank (3,000+ questions), and 2 mock exams are all included in your exam fee at no extra cost. Candidates who use these fully can reduce or eliminate the need for paid third-party materials.

Payment method for CFA fees from India

CFA Institute accepts: international credit cards (Visa/Mastercard — must be enabled for international transactions), debit cards (if international use enabled), and wire transfer. Most Indian candidates pay via international credit card or a forex card. The fee is charged in USD — your bank applies the prevailing exchange rate on the date of transaction.

Note on INR estimates: The INR values in this guide are approximate based on USD/INR ~83.5. Actual INR amount will vary by the rate on your payment date. Always check current rates before budgeting.
